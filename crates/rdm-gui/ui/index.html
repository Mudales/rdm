Browser extension

The rdm extension sends links, videos and captured downloads to this app through a small helper (rdm-host). The setup assistant adds both to a browser in a few clicks.

ffmpeg

Streams (HLS/DASH) with a single track are saved without extra tools. ffmpeg is used to merge separate video and audio, to turn MPEG-TS into MP4, and to extract MP3/M4A audio.

Videos on web pages (yt-dlp)

yt-dlp finds the video on pages from YouTube, Vimeo and over a thousand other sites; rdm then downloads it with several connections. YouTube also needs Deno, a JavaScript runtime. DRM-protected videos are never downloaded.
